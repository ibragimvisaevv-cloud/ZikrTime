# Living Horizon — Android 0.8.8

Seven individually generated background assets, created with the built-in ImageGen tool. No collages, downloaded stock images, generated text, or generated prayer times are used. Original 1536×1024 images are encoded as WebP (quality 86) without changing their proportions. Compose crops uniformly and protects real translated text with scrims. The scene is fictional, not a location claim. All images are bundled for offline use.

The visual direction is emerald, sage, warm ivory and muted gold. Home uses a six-cell timetable; Knowledge uses two distinct panoramic remembrance cards and topic tiles; Focus, Tasbih and Shop have their own scenes. Settings use grouped destination tiles. Manual appearance and system appearance remain available. Existing Arabic text and religious data are kept as text.
